# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Self-directed learners studying a topic on their own — the project owner and
future learners. They work alone, on a laptop, in long focused sessions: reading
AI-generated explanations, marking doubts, asking quick questions, practicing.
A personal local learning lab, not a shared/SaaS service.

## Product Purpose

LLL (LifeLongLearn) turns a topic into a living study artifact — an intro
assessment, a multi-page explain tutorial, progressive practice, extensions, and
a summary. Claude Code agents generate and revise the artifact; the learner
reads, questions, and iterates. Success = the learner understands the topic
deeply and keeps a durable, improvable study record.

## Brand Personality

Warm, calm, scholarly, encouraging. A "thinking partner," not an answer
dispenser (iteration-05 ethos). Patient, honest about uncertainty, never cold or
corporate.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS dashboards: navy/indigo, `#4c6ef5`-style blues, gradient hero metrics.
- Cold corporate LMS / enterprise learning UIs.
- The flat all-gray, low-contrast "AI default."
- Italic body text (the user dislikes italic; emphasis is conveyed by weight).

## Design Principles

- Calm reading first: the study page is the hero; chrome recedes.
- Warm and handcrafted, not sterile: cream paper, sage accent, soft warm shadows.
- One accent (sage) used with restraint; warm neutrals carry the surface.
- Show thinking, invite questioning: surfaces make uncertainty and revision feel normal.
- Honest density: enough information per screen, generous whitespace, no card-grid filler.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Body text ≥4.5:1 on the cream paper — the muted tone must stay readable (verify; bump toward ink if close).
- `prefers-reduced-motion` respected on every animation.
- No italic emphasis (weight only); the global italic-disable stays.
- Bilingual copy (Chinese + English); PingFang SC / Microsoft YaHei fallbacks kept.
